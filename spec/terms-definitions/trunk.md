[[def: trunk, trunks]]

~ The undisputed path through a [[ref: KEL]]. A KEL is a directed acyclic graph
~    of events, and a [[ref: superseding recovery]] creates a branch in it; the
~    trunk is the single path that reconciliation establishes, and every
~    superseded branch is disputed. This specification derives the DID document,
~    and evaluates every other requirement of
~    [Read (Resolve)](#read-resolve), over the trunk.

~ More detail: the [KERI specification](#KSWG-KERI), §Superseding Recovery and
~    Reconciliation.
