[[def: irreconcilable-fork, irreconcilable fork, irreconcilable]]

~ A dispute in a [[ref: KEL]] that the superseding acceptance rules do not
~    resolve, so that no [[ref: trunk]] can be established. KERI states the
~    condition as validators being unable to universally find the same
~    undisputed path through the KEL. A [[ref: superseding recovery]] is the
~    opposite case: it forks the KEL and is reconcilable, and this
~    specification requires resolution to continue through it.

~ More detail: the [KERI specification](#KSWG-KERI), §Superseding Recovery and
~    Reconciliation.
